Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Weekly Docket is free by email every week. The Brief is paid for by its monthly and every-two-weeks issues, which carry the full numbers behind many of these filings.

Why don't you name the people involved?

We print businesses, public bodies and addresses. We don't print private people: not buyers, sellers, applicants or residents. A request a resident makes about their own home is counted, not named.

How current is it?

Town agendas and minutes are read the week they post. County deeds reach the public file about ten days after they are recorded, and the county's public health inspection file runs further behind; each item carries the date of its record, so you always know how fresh it is.

What if a week is quiet?

Then the issue is short. We don't fill space.
